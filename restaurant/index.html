Our Roots

Bringing the rich heritage of Saurashtra to your plate.

At Kathiyawadi Rajwadi, we celebrate the vibrant, spicy, and soulful flavors of Gujarat. Our recipes have been passed down through generations, utilizing traditional clay ovens and hand-ground spices. From our smoky Ringan no Olo to our sweet Mohanthal, every bite is a journey to the heart of India.
